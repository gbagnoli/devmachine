SSH_AUTH_SOCK is unset or empty: no SSH agent is advertised to Skillet.
In your current shell, start an agent and load your private key:
  eval "$(ssh-agent -s)"
  ssh-add /path/to/your/key
Enter the key passphrase in your terminal, then retry from that same shell.
Setup: ublue/skillet/crates/workstation/README.md#ssh-agent
